Section 01

Who We Are

Byrsa ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile security application developed to protect Android users against cloud hacking, zero-click attacks, and authentication token theft. Byrsa is designed from the ground up to operate entirely offline, with no dependency on external servers or cloud infrastructure.

This Privacy Policy explains how Byrsa handles information when you use our application. Given the nature of our architecture — fully local, no backend — this policy is notably short: we simply do not collect your data.

Section 03

How Your Data Is Stored Locally

All data generated by Byrsa — security events, scan results, AI insights, settings, and protected app configurations — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device. This database is encrypted using AES-256 in production builds.

Android Keystore: Your encryption keys are stored in the Android Keystore system, a hardware-backed secure enclave. Keys cannot be extracted from the device, even by Byrsa itself.

When you uninstall Byrsa, all locally stored data is permanently deleted. There is no remote backup of your Byrsa data because no remote storage exists.

Section 05

In-App Purchases

Byrsa offers optional Premium subscriptions (Monthly, Annual, Lifetime) processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We do not handle, see, or store your payment information. All purchase validation occurs between your device and Google's servers using Google Play's native SDK.

Your purchase status is stored locally on your device. No purchase history is sent to Byrsa servers — because Byrsa has no servers.

Section 06

Third-Party Services

Byrsa does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data monetization services. We do not use:

No Firebase Analytics · No Google Analytics · No Facebook SDK · No Crashlytics · No Amplitude · No Mixpanel · No advertising SDKs of any kind.

The only external service involved is Google Play Billing for in-app purchase processing, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Section 07

Children's Privacy

Byrsa does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. As Byrsa collects no personal data from any user regardless of age, the app is inherently compliant with COPPA and similar regulations protecting children's privacy.

Section 08

Your Rights

Because Byrsa collects no personal data, most traditional privacy rights (access, rectification, deletion, portability) are automatically satisfied — there is simply no data about you held by us to access, correct, or delete.

To delete all data associated with Byrsa, simply uninstall the application from your device. All local data is permanently erased upon uninstallation.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify users of material changes through an in-app notification. The effective date at the top of this document will always reflect the most recent revision.

Continued use of Byrsa after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
